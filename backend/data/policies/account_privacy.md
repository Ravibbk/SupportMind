# NovaCart Account Security, Data Privacy & GDPR Rights

*Last Updated: February 5, 2026*

## 1. Account Access & Password Security
- Customers are responsible for safeguarding login credentials. NovaCart supports Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via SMS or authenticator apps (TOTP).
- After **5 consecutive failed login attempts**, an account is locked for 30 minutes for security prevention. You can trigger an instant unlock via the automated password reset email.
- SupportMind agents will **never** ask you for your raw account password, OTP code, or full credit card CVV.

## 2. Personal Data Protection & PII Standards
- We strictly adhere to CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) and GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) frameworks.
- NovaCart does not sell, lease, or monetize your personal shopping history or contact data to third-party data brokers.
- Payment card data is tokenized using PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant vaulting; NovaCart servers do not store raw 16-digit card numbers.

## 3. Account Deletion & Right to Be Forgotten
- You can request complete erasure of your personal data by filing an "Account Deletion Request" through SupportMind or under **Account Settings > Privacy Controls**.
- Erasure requests are processed within **30 calendar days**.
- Note: Certain invoice records must be retained in an anonymized archive for up to 7 years to satisfy federal statutory tax regulations.

## 4. Email Preferences & Marketing Opt-Out
- You can toggle newsletter, promotional discounts, and SMS marketing subscriptions under **Account > Communication Preferences**.
- Transactional messages (order receipts, shipping milestones, security alerts) cannot be disabled as they are required for order fulfillment.
