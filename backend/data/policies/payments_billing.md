# NovaCart Payments, Invoicing & Billing Terms

*Last Updated: January 10, 2026*

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
NovaCart accepts a wide variety of secure payment methods:
- Credit and Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and JCB.
- Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal One-Touch.
- Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL): Klarna and Afterpay (available for eligible orders between $35.00 and $1,500.00).
- NovaCart Gift Cards and Reward Points balance.
- We do not accept personal checks, money orders, cash on delivery (COD), or direct cryptocurrency payments.

## 2. Authorization Holds vs. Actual Charges
- When you click "Place Order", your financial institution places a temporary **pre-authorization hold** on your account for the order total.
- Your card is **officially charged** only when the items physically depart our distribution center.
- If an order is canceled prior to shipment, the authorization hold drops off automatically according to your bank's schedule (typically 2 to 5 business days).

## 3. Sales Tax & Duties
- Sales tax is calculated automatically at checkout based on your state, county, and municipal jurisdiction in accordance with prevailing local tax legislation.
- International orders may incur import tariffs, customs processing duties, and value-added tax (VAT) imposed by the destination nation. These duties are collected at checkout when DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) is selected.

## 4. Invoices, Receipts & Duplicate Charges
- Tax invoices and receipt PDFs can be downloaded directly from your **Account > Order History** section at any time.
- If you notice what appears to be a **duplicate charge** on your bank statement, it is nearly always a temporary authorization hold alongside the finalized charge. If both persist for more than 5 business days, notify SupportMind immediately with your statement screenshot.

## 5. Billing Disputes & Chargeback Policy
- We encourage customers to reach out to SupportMind before initiating a dispute with their credit card provider. Our agents can resolve refund issues in under 24 hours without the 60-90 day bank dispute investigation delay.
