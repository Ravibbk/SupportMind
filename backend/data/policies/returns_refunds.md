# NovaCart Return & Refund Policy

*Last Updated: January 15, 2026*

## 1. 30-Day Standard Return Window
At NovaCart, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a **30-day return window** for most items from the date of confirmed delivery.
- Items must be returned in their original condition, unwashed, unworn, with all original tags attached and packaging intact.
- Items returned between 31 and 45 days are eligible for **store credit only**, subject to a 10% restocking fee.
- Returns requested beyond 45 days from delivery cannot be accepted.

## 2. Non-Returnable & Final Sale Items
The following items are strictly non-returnable:
- Personalized, custom-engraved, or made-to-order merchandise.
- Perishable goods, grocery items, and live plants.
- Opened intimate apparel, swimwear (without hygiene seals), and personal wellness devices.
- Downloadable digital software licenses and gift cards.
- Items explicitly designated as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" on the product detail page.

## 3. Refund Processing Timelines & Methods
Once your return parcel arrives at our fulfillment facility:
- Our quality inspection team processes the item within **2 to 4 business days**.
- Approved refunds are credited back to the **original payment method**:
  - Credit/Debit Card: 3 to 5 business days depending on your issuing bank.
  - PayPal / Digital Wallets: Within 24 hours of approval.
  - Store Credit / NovaPoints: Instantly credited to your NovaCart account.
- Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to a NovaCart fulfillment error or defective item.

## 4. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items
If you received an item that is defective, broken in transit, or incorrect:
- You must report the issue within **7 days of delivery** via the SupportMind portal or support ticket.
- Provide clear photographic evidence of the item, shipping packaging, and packing slip.
- NovaCart will issue an immediate prepaid expedited replacement or 100% full refund including all shipping fees.

## 5. Return Shipping Fees
- Domestic US returns using our prepaid Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) label incur a flat **$5.99 return shipping deduction** from the refund balance.
- Return shipping is **100% free** for NovaCart Plus members or when selecting store credit as the refund method.
