# NovaCart Promotional Codes, Coupons & Loyalty Points

*Last Updated: February 12, 2026*

## 1. Terms of Promotional Discount Codes
- Promotional codes are case-sensitive and must be applied prior to clicking "Place Order".
- Discount codes cannot be retroactively applied to orders already submitted or dispatched.
- Only **one promotional code** may be redeemed per transaction. Discounts cannot be combined or "stacked" with flash-sale doorbusters or employee clearance codes.

## 2. Minimum Spend & Brand Restrictions
- Certain coupons require a minimum merchandise subtotal before taxes and shipping fees (e.g., "$15 off orders over $75").
- Brand exclusions: Apple, Sony, Dyson, and Bose hardware are excluded from standard percent-off promotional campaigns unless specifically explicitly branded as eligible.

## 3. First-Order Welcome Discount
- New customers receive 10% off their first order by subscribing to the NovaCart weekly dispatch using code `WELCOME10`.
- The code is single-use per household and requires customer account creation.

## 4. Expired or Invalid Codes
- If a promotional code displays *"Invalid or Expired Code"*:
  - Verify character spelling (watch out for `0` vs `O`).
  - Confirm that the promo expiration date in EST has not elapsed.
  - Check that the cart total satisfies minimum spend criteria.
  - If a valid promotional email was received within 24 hours, contact SupportMind with the promo campaign title.
