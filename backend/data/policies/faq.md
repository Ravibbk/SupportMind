# NovaCart Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

*Last Updated: February 10, 2026*

## 1. How do I track my active order?
You can track your order status in real time by clicking the tracking link in your dispatch email, or by asking the SupportMind Copilot: *"What is the status of order ORD-XXXXX?"* or providing your registered account email.

## 2. Does NovaCart ship to APO/FPO or PO Boxes?
Yes! Standard Ground shipping is available for all US PO Boxes and military APO/FPO/DPO addresses via USPS Priority Mail. Overnight and 2-Day expedited shipping options are not available for PO Box addresses.

## 3. What is NovaCart Plus?
NovaCart Plus is our premium VIP membership program priced at **$79.00/year**:
- Free 2-Day expedited shipping on all orders with zero minimum purchase.
- Free returns with no RMA label fees.
- 5% cash-back reward points on all electronics and home goods.
- Dedicated 24/7 priority live customer service routing.

## 4. How do I apply a discount code?
Enter your coupon code in the **"Promo Code / Gift Card"** box at the checkout review step and click Apply before completing payment. Promo codes cannot be stacked unless explicitly advertised.

## 5. Can I change my delivery address after shipping?
Once shipped, address changes must be handled through the carrier (UPS My Choice or FedEx Delivery Manager) for local locker hold. NovaCart cannot redirect in-flight packages directly.
