# NovaCart Warranty & Product Repair Guarantee

*Last Updated: January 20, 2026*

## 1. 1-Year Limited Hardware Guarantee
NovaCart provides a complimentary **1-Year Limited Manufacturer & Merchant Warranty** on all electronic items, appliances, audio equipment, and smart home hardware purchased directly on our platform.
- The warranty activates automatically on the confirmed date of product delivery.
- Coverage applies exclusively to the original purchaser and is non-transferable.

## 2. What Is Covered
- Defective electronic components, faulty soldering, or circuit board failure under normal operation.
- Battery degradation exceeding 30% capacity loss within the first 6 months.
- Unresponsive touchscreens, buttons, switches, and factory sensor malfunctions.
- Structural manufacturing flaws in materials or workmanship.

## 3. What Is Excluded
- Accidental drops, screen cracks, water damage, liquid spills, or liquid submersion.
- Cosmetic scratches, dents, and standard wear-and-tear resulting from prolonged use.
- Unauthorized third-party repairs, firmware modifications, jailbreaking, or disassembly.
- Commercial or industrial usage of consumer-grade consumer equipment.
- Acts of nature (lightning surges, floods, earthquakes).

## 4. Warranty Claim Procedure
To file a warranty service claim:
1. Submit a request through the SupportMind portal with your Order ID and serial number.
2. Provide a 15-30 second video demonstrating the technical malfunction.
3. Our technical team evaluates the ticket within **48 hours**.
4. If approved, NovaCart issues a prepaid shipping label to our centralized repair lab.
5. We will repair the item, replace it with an equivalent recertified unit, or issue a full refund if parts are unavailable.
