# NovaCart Order Cancellation & Modification Policy

*Last Updated: January 25, 2026*

## 1. Cancellation Window
- You may cancel an order free of charge within **60 minutes** of submitting your order online.
- During this window, click **Cancel Order** in your order confirmation email or under **Account > Active Orders**.
- After 60 minutes, the order batch transmits to automated picking systems at our robotic distribution hub, and automated online cancellation is no longer possible.

## 2. Order Modification (Items, Colors, Sizes)
- Because fulfillment systems are real-time, individual items, quantities, or product variants cannot be altered once placed.
- To change items within the 60-minute grace period: cancel the existing order and place a fresh order with your desired specifications.

## 3. Cancellations by NovaCart
NovaCart reserves the right to cancel orders under specific circumstances:
- Pricing glitches or severe typographical listing errors.
- Unanticipated warehouse inventory stockouts (a full refund is issued immediately with a 15% goodwill coupon).
- Fraud detection flags raised by our automated payment risk scoring engine.

## 4. Refused Delivery & Undeliverable Packages
- If a customer intentionally refuses delivery of a parcel, or if 3 delivery attempts fail and the parcel returns to sender:
- A refund is issued minus a **$9.99 return transit handling fee**.
