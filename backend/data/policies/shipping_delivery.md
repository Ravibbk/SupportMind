# NovaCart Shipping & Delivery Guidelines

*Last Updated: February 1, 2026*

## 1. Shipping Speeds & Rates
NovaCart partners with premier global carriers including FedEx, UPS, DHL Express, and USPS to provide dependable delivery.

| Shipping Tier | Estimated Delivery Time | Standard Fee | Free Shipping Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard Ground** | 3 - 5 business days | $4.99 | Orders over $50.00 |
| **Expedited 2-Day** | 2 business days | $12.99 | Free for NovaCart Plus |
| **Overnight Express** | 1 business day (order by 1 PM EST) | $24.99 | Not eligible for waivers |
| **International Priority** | 7 - 14 business days | Calculated at checkout | Not eligible |

## 2. Order Processing & Dispatch
- Orders placed Monday through Friday before **2:00 PM EST** are processed and dispatched the same day.
- Orders submitted after 2:00 PM EST, on weekends, or during public federal holidays ship the following business day.
- Tracking numbers are generated and sent via email within 2 hours of warehouse dispatch.

## 3. Delayed, Stalled, or Lost Packages
- A package is considered **delayed** if tracking shows no milestone scan for more than 48 consecutive hours past the estimated delivery window.
- If your parcel is delayed:
  - Check the carrier's real-time portal for weather disruptions or transit hub congestion.
  - If a domestic parcel has not moved for 5 full business days, SupportMind can trigger a carrier investigation or initiate a complimentary replacement dispatch.
- **Stolen / Missing Packages Marked Delivered**:
  - Please check porch hiding spots, back doors, and neighbors first.
  - If still missing after 24 hours of "Delivered" status, file a Missing Package claim with SupportMind within 14 calendar days.

## 4. Address Corrections & Delivery Rerouting
- Shipping addresses can be modified online within **45 minutes** of order placement before warehouse fulfillment locks the batch.
- Once an order status is "In Transit", carriers may support rerouting to a local pickup locker (FedEx Hold at Location / UPS Access Point) for a $6.00 carrier administrative fee.
